# Knowledge Intelligence (KI) — Product Brief

Positioning: From Institutional Memory to National Intelligence.
Product principle: Remember · Understand · Anticipate.
Current market scope: Private-sector organizations, universities, research centers, consultants and experts. Government customers are explicitly out of the current scope.

## Product definition
KI is a customizable enterprise knowledge-intelligence platform. Each customer operates in a logically isolated knowledge environment. Customers may exchange explicitly approved knowledge assets with other organizations in a later, governed ecosystem.

## Deployment
- KI Cloud: multi-tenant SaaS with strict tenant isolation.
- KI Private: dedicated or customer-managed deployment.

The application is one product with two deployment modes. Keep it model-agnostic through a provider gateway. No customer content may be used to train public or shared models without explicit, informed permission.

## MVP promise
Turn an organization's documents and recorded experience into a searchable, source-grounded knowledge base.

### MVP capabilities
1. Organization and workspace onboarding.
2. User identity, roles and workspace-level authorization.
3. Document upload, extraction, indexing and version tracking.
4. Arabic/English semantic retrieval.
5. AI question answering with verifiable source citations.
6. Explicit refusal when evidence is missing or access is denied.
7. Audit-ready access and ingestion events.
8. Tenant-aware administration and usage visibility.

### Not in MVP
- Cross-company knowledge exchange.
- Predictive decision simulation.
- Automated employee performance or ranking.
- Government integrations or government-specific claims.
- Autonomous actions that modify business records.
- Claims of compliance or certification without independent assessment.

## Product modules
- KI Memory: institutional memory and document lifecycle.
- KI Ingestion: document and knowledge intake.
- KI Intelligence: grounded Q&A, synthesis and classification.
- KI Graph: relationships among projects, decisions, people and evidence.
- KI Experts: consent-based expertise profiles and knowledge handover.
- KI Decision: decision rationale and outcome history.
- KI Foresight: knowledge gaps, concentration risk and early signals.
- KI Exchange: permissioned knowledge sharing in a later phase.

## Success measures
- Retrieval precision and answer faithfulness on a reviewed benchmark.
- Percentage of answers with valid source citations.
- Correct refusal rate for unsupported questions.
- Cross-tenant access isolation test results.
- Time to ingest and retrieve a representative knowledge collection.
- Active use and repeat usage by pilot teams.

## Pilot validation
Run discovery with 3–5 design-partner organizations. Validate high-value workflows, data sensitivity, deployment preference, integrations, willingness to pay and measurable time saved. Do not present assumptions as market facts.
